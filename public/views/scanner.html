Student Pass Scanner (Kiosk Mode)

Point your camera at a student's Digital MAC Pass QR code to verify device identity and mark attendance.

💡 How this works:

Students display their Digital Pass on their phone screen. The camera scans the encoded signature, maps their Roll Number and hardware MAC, and validates their presence against the local active session.
